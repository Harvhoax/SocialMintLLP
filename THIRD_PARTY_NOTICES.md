Source: https://github.com/bizarro/infinite-circular-webgl-gallery

The full MIT notice is distributed in `public/licenses/infinite-circular-gallery-MIT.txt`. SocialMint uses Three.js for the same rendering approach, supplies its own films and artwork, and adds single-video preview playback, native expanded viewing, reduced-motion fallback, and resource cleanup.

Source: https://github.com/clementgrellier/gradientslider

The full MIT notice is distributed in `public/licenses/gradientslider-MIT.txt`.

SocialMint adaptations include React lifecycle cleanup, accessible controls, native vertical page scrolling, readable service details outside the transformed artwork, and no card blur or opacity fading.
